# Advertise with us: opening it to external advertisers

## Summary
Companies, schools, and sponsors who are not ICF members can sign in with Google, Microsoft, Apple, or email. They complete a short organisation profile once, then use the same `/advertise` wizard and dashboard as members. ICF members keep their current path. The synced member records stay untouched.

## What users see
- **Signed-out visitor at `/advertise`:** a public page with the packages, a short summary of the rate card, the specs, and one clear "Book a placement" button.
- **Sign-in:** a new advertiser screen with Continue with Google, Microsoft, or Apple, plus email and password sign-up with confirmation. It links to member sign-in for ICF members.
- **First visit after sign-in:** if the account is neither a member nor an advertiser yet, a short profile step asks for the organisation name, billing address (country, postcode, city, street), contact name, phone, and an optional VAT number. Saving it gives the account advertiser access.
- **Advertiser view:** the wizard and "My requests" dashboard inside a slim advertiser frame with its own header and sign-out. Advertisers see no member navigation and cannot open Member Area pages.
- **Members:** nothing changes. `/advertise` keeps the member frame.
- **Staff:** the ad-request inbox shows whether each requester is a "Member" or an "External advertiser", with the organisation profile. Pricing for external advertisers is the non-member price, which staff can adjust when they review.
