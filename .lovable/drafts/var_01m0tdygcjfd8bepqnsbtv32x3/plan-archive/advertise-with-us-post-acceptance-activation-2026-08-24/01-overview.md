# Advertise with us — post-acceptance activation

The advertising feature is code-complete. What is left is turning it on once this draft is accepted: confirm the price list that ships with the change, correct the numbers if the board's rate card differs, and put the nightly housekeeping job on a schedule so placements retire themselves and advertisers get their 30-day warning.

Nothing here changes the member or staff experience. It is configuration and one scheduled job.

## What ships automatically on accept

| Silver package | 12 months / 6 months | 2000 / 1150 |
